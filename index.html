Roofing AI collects information voluntarily provided through customer inquiries, including names, phone numbers, property addresses, roofing issues, and preferred appointment times. This information is used to respond to inquiries and help coordinate roofing service requests.

Mobile information and SMS opt-in consent will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Service providers may process information as needed to operate the messaging service.
